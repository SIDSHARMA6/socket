Privacy Policy – HideMaster

Last updated: January 2026

HideMaster is a Safari web extension designed to help users hide unwanted elements and block distracting pages while browsing.

Data Collection

HideMaster does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

Browsing Data

All actions performed by HideMaster (such as hiding elements or blocking pages) are processed locally on your device. No browsing history, website data, or personal information is sent to any server.

Third-Party Services

HideMaster does not use third-party analytics, tracking tools, or advertising services.

Permissions

The extension requires access to webpage content only to perform its intended functionality. This access is used solely on the user’s device.

Data Sharing

No data is shared with third parties.

Changes

If this privacy policy changes, updates will be reflected on this page.
